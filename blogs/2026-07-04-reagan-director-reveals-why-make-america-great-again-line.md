The re-release of the "Reagan" movie includes a clip featuring a famous line from the 40th president that was left out of the original. In the scene, Dennis Quaid’s President Ronald Reagan gives a speech at the Building and Construction Trades Department, AFL-CIO. "You and your forebearers helped build this nation," he says in the clip. "Now help us rebuild it. And together, we will make America great again."
